Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you'd want to know before we start working together

How long does SEO take to show results?

Most clients start seeing movement in rankings and traffic within 6–8 weeks, with stronger, compounding results between month 3 and month 6 depending on competition and the site's starting point.

Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?

Yes — I currently work with clients across Pakistan, the UAE, the USA, the UK, and South Africa, all managed remotely with regular updates and clear written reports.

What's included in a technical SEO audit?

A full site crawl and broken-link check, Core Web Vitals and speed review, indexing and sitemap validation, structured data checks, and a prioritized action plan you can hand straight to your developer.

Do you offer one-time projects or ongoing retainers?

Both. One-time audits and on-page fixes work well for smaller sites, while ongoing monthly retainers suit e-commerce and content-heavy sites that need continuous optimization and reporting.

How do you report progress?

You'll get a clear monthly dashboard covering rankings, organic traffic, and conversions, plus a short written summary explaining what changed and what's planned next.

What do you need from me to get started?

Just access to your website's analytics and search console (or help setting them up), plus a short call to understand your business goals. From there, I handle the rest.
